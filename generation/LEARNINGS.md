# Generation learnings

Living log of what gets confirmed while generating video in Blender. This file is not a style rule. The craft lives in the other files in `generation/`:

- camera, scale, continuity, character, environment, actions, extras → `scene-design.md`
- light, materials, atmosphere, time of day, weather → `look-and-lighting.md`
- sound → `audio.md`
- how a job runs, QC, delivery, a correction on a delivered version → `workflow-and-qc.md`
- a Blender call or a machine fact that was actually verified → `blender-notes.md`

A line moves into the file that owns the topic only after Daniel confirms it. Until then it stays here, marked **NOT A RULE**. Things not yet seen in a render, a script run, or his note are marked **ASSUMPTION** or **UNKNOWN**.

Append a fact in the same pass it is learned. Do not wait for the end of the job. Do not invent a session. This log starts with no sessions.

The teaching method (operate the scene in Blender; retain the character model; the planned training loop) is already stated in `README.md`. It is not re-logged here as a fake session. Do not paste the locked standing generation benchmark, or the from-scratch generation benchmark, into an entry. Name them. Record measurements and flags only.

## How a fact gets here

Confirmed means one of: a render was compared to the locked wording and the match was checked on the frames, the saved scene showed the camera and the actions, a Blender run printed the value, or Daniel stated it. A guess about what a frame probably shows is not enough.
