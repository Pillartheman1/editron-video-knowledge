# Audio (generation)

Generated and designed sound for this wing. Parallel to the editing wing's audio file, and it does not inherit that wing's music, loudness targets, or client mixes.

No generation sound has been confirmed. Loudness, sample rate, and a house effects level are **UNKNOWN** until Daniel states them. Honor the brief in front of you. Do not invent a score or a soundscape to finish a picture.

## 1. What the brief says
- The audio brief is part of the lock. Comparable runs keep it byte-for-byte, the same way they keep the picture prompt (`workflow-and-qc.md`).
- Build only the layers the brief names. A layer added because a picture "usually has one" is an extra.
- In the delivery report, say whether each named layer matched, and list any layer that was not in the brief.

## 2. Music
- **Honor a no-music brief.** When the brief says no music, the deliverable has no score, no sting, and no quiet bed.
- When the brief names music, use the track it names, from the start point it names, through the end it names.
- Do not pick a song he did not name. A house music taste for generation is **awaiting confirmed notes**.

## 3. Effects
- Rain, storm, footsteps, and other effects are in the mix only when the brief specifies them, and only for the event on screen.
- Picture and sound agree. Footsteps without steps, or rain in the track with a clear sky in the frames, is a miss (`look-and-lighting.md` §5).
- Level, timing, and how many layers may stack are **awaiting confirmed notes**. Until then, use the count and the level the brief states. If it states none, record the choice as **ASSUMPTION** and hold it across comparable runs.

## 4. Sources and licenses
- **No copyrighted official sound-library rips.** Do not take effects from official, studio, or commercial SFX libraries unless the brief licenses that library for this job and the report names the license.
- A generated or designed sound is allowed when the brief asks for designed sound. Record how it was made.
- A file whose license is unknown stays out. Do not drop it in `assets/` to "sort out later."

## 5. Sync
- An effect that belongs to an action lands on the frame where that action happens in the operated scene. The scene's action is the reference, because the action was keyframed there (`scene-design.md` §6).
- If the brief is silent on sync, flag the placement as **ASSUMPTION**.
